# Test checklist for 0.1

Play through these and report anything that doesn't match. The most useful
details are:

- which step it was,
- what you saw,
- and the last lines of `Documents\RDR2 Outlaw Mode\outlawmode.log`.

Load the **slot 6 save** (the 100% epilogue save) before you start.

## 1. Mod loads
- [ ] Press F5. A red "Outlaw Mode 0.1.0" menu appears at the top left.
- [ ] Arrow keys move the highlight, and Backspace goes back.
- [ ] Set Outlaw Mode to On. A message confirms it.

## 2. Arthur
- [ ] Within a second or two, you turn into Arthur.
- [ ] Menu > Arthur > Outfit: Left and Right change his clothes.
- [ ] Save the game, reload it, and confirm you're still Arthur.
- [ ] Die once, and confirm you respawn as Arthur.
- [ ] Do your guns and satchel still work? Note anything missing.

## 3. Camp
- [ ] Menu > Camp > Pitch camp here. A campfire, 4 tents, and bedrolls appear, along with a tent icon on the map.
- [ ] Menu > Camp > Move camp to a known spot > pick one. The camp moves there.
- [ ] Ride more than 120m away, then use Camp > Ride back to camp.
- [ ] Stand by the fire and choose Rest by the fire. Time should skip ahead and your cores should fill.

## 4. Gang
- [ ] Menu > Gang > Dutch > Recruit. Dutch appears behind you with a horse.
- [ ] Recruit 2 or 3 more people.
- [ ] Mount your horse. They should mount theirs and ride with you.
- [ ] Get off your horse. They should dismount nearby.
- [ ] F7 makes them wait, and F6 brings them back.
- [ ] Aim at a hostile NPC and press F8. They should attack.
- [ ] Send one of them to camp, then ride to camp. They should be at the fire.
- [ ] Do any of them show up invisible, naked, or as the wrong person? Name who.

## 5. Guarma
- [ ] Go to the Saint Denis harbor pier, near the boat icon on the map. A "Travel available" hint should show at the bottom of the screen.
- [ ] Menu > Travel > Book passage to Guarma. You should arrive on Guarma with a working map and normal-looking sea.
- [ ] Book passage back to Saint Denis from the Guarma pier.

## 6. Colter
- [ ] In Valentine, open Menu > Travel > Hire a carriage to Colter.
- [ ] Colter should have townsfolk, a campfire, and a storekeeper with a shop icon.
- [ ] Walk up to the storekeeper, then open Menu > Colter General Store and buy ammo and a meal.
- [ ] Hire a carriage back.
