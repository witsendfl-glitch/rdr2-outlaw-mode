# RDR2 "Outlaw Mode" plan (draft, agreed with Chase 2026-10-25)

Assumes PC, script mod on Script Hook RDR2. Separate mode starts from a post-epilogue save; play as Arthur (no TB), no story missions, own save file and menu.

1. **Foundation + feasibility tests**: mode toggle (save/hotkey, not main menu), own save data and menu. Test Guarma passage from Saint Denis docks, and populating Colter (people, stores, carriage to it).
2. **Followers and camp**: recruit original gang members (no loyalty system), follow/wait/guard/attack commands; place camp at Online camp locations of your choice.
3. **Gang wars**: rival gangs (more Italian mob in Saint Denis) fight over territory; territory map menu; hideouts to clear/retake/upgrade for income.
4. **Money and heists**: buy/upgrade homestead, ranch, or saloon with livestock, hired hands, passive income; plannable bank and train heists that refresh, crewed by followers.

Dropped: ironclad. Dropped: follower loyalty.
